# Internal Source-of-Wealth Policy (SOW-POL)

**FICTIONAL. Written for a portfolio project. Not the policy of any real institution and not legal or compliance advice.**

Clause references take the form `SOW-POL 4.2`. Thresholds below are the same ones the review system's rule table applies. Amounts are compared at nominal value in the document's stated currency.

## 1. Purpose
1.1 This policy sets what a reviewer needs before accepting a client's stated source of wealth.

1.2 Every conclusion must trace to a verbatim quote from a client document or to a clause of this policy.

## 2. Required content by document type
2.1 Bank statement: client name, issuing bank, statement period, currency, closing balance, and at least one material transaction.

2.2 Share sale agreement: client name, buyer, agreement date, currency, consideration.

2.3 Trust deed or distribution letter: client name, trustee, document date, currency, distributed amount.

2.4 Salary letter: client name, employer, letter date, currency, annual remuneration.

2.5 Gift letter: client name, donor, letter date, currency, gift amount.

2.6 A document missing any required item, or stating an item without a supporting quote, is `insufficient`. The reviewer requests a complete document and draws no conclusion from the incomplete one.

## 3. Unexplained credits
3.1 A single credit of 50,000 or more with no identifiable counterparty requires follow-up. The client must provide the origin of the funds and a supporting document, such as a sale completion statement or a withdrawal record.

3.2 Credits below 50,000 do not trigger clause 3.1 on their own.

## 4. Gifts
4.1 A gift below 100,000 is acceptable on a signed donor declaration stating the amount, the date, and the relationship to the client.

4.2 A gift of 100,000 or more requires follow-up. In addition to the declaration in clause 4.1, the reviewer must obtain all of:
(a) a bank statement of the donor showing the funds leaving the donor's account;
(b) a document evidencing the origin of the donor's funds, such as a property sale completion statement, a share sale agreement, or a salary letter;
(c) evidence of the relationship between donor and client.

4.3 A donor's statement about the origin of funds, without a document, does not satisfy clause 4.2(b).

## 5. Unclear or contradictory content
5.1 Any contradiction or unclear item recorded during extraction requires follow-up, including deferred or conditional payments with no firm date and amounts stated in more than one currency.

## 6. Sanctions screening and Enhanced Due Diligence
6.1 The client and the counterparty are screened against the sanctions list on every review.

6.2 Any match, including an alias or a reordered name, is escalated to Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) regardless of the quality of the documents.

6.3 Escalation to EDD requires confirmation by a human reviewer. The system may recommend it but may not complete it alone.
